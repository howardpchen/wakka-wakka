# Store listing draft

## Name

Wakka Wakka

## Short description

Tilt your wrist, sweep a scrolling maze, and outsmart four colorful pursuers.

## Long description

Wakka Wakka is a compact maze-chase game designed for Pebble Time 2. Guide a
quick golden runner through a scrolling stage by tilting your wrist. Clear every
energy dot while four colorful rivals use different pursuit strategies to close
in.

Large energy dots briefly reverse the chase: rivals turn blue and become
vulnerable, giving you a chance to hunt them for bonus points. Three lives, a
clear ready prompt, responsive buffered turns, a wraparound tunnel, and an
optional gameplay backlight make each run easy to start and difficult to master.

Wakka Wakka runs entirely on the watch. It needs no account, configuration,
network connection, or phone service during play.

## Controls

- Tilt: buffer turns at the next legal junction
- UP: toggle backlight
- DOWN: south-direction emulator fallback
- SELECT: start/restart or pause/resume and recalibrate
- BACK: exit

Hold the watch level during calibration.

## Compatibility

- Pebble Time 2 only
- Platform: Emery
- Requires accelerometer/IMU access
- No runtime phone connection, account, settings page, or network access
- English interface
