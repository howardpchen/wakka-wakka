# Support information

## Required before submission

- Public support email: `acupojava@yahoo.com`
- Support URL or issue tracker: none
- Publisher/developer display name: `acupojava`

Do not substitute the Pebble developer-account email automatically; the support
address will be visible publicly.

## Suggested support response scope

Support requests should include:

- Pebble model and firmware version
- Wakka Wakka version
- What happened immediately before the issue
- Whether the watch app exited or the entire watch rebooted
- Whether the issue repeats after relaunch
