# Original branding and artwork review

## Clear for use

- Name: `Wakka Wakka` does not use the name of another maze-chase franchise.
- Launcher icon: original cyan-corridor/yellow-comet/four-spark composition,
  generated specifically for this project.
- Store copy: does not name or claim affiliation with another game.
- Audio: no copied music or sound effects are included.
- Maze: project-specific 19×21 topology, not a copied arcade maze.

## Artwork decision

The in-game visuals use familiar geometric genre shorthand:

- a round yellow player with a mouth-like line;
- rounded, skirt-bottom pursuer silhouettes with paired eyes;
- large corner energy dots and blue vulnerable pursuers in the familiar
  maze-chase arrangement.

These are primitive geometric shapes rather than copied bitmap assets. hc
accepted the existing artwork for the public release. Public terminology is
standardized on runner, rivals, and energy dots.

## Avoid in public materials

- Names belonging to other maze-chase games or their characters
- Copied character silhouettes, maze layouts, sounds, logos, or typography
- Claims that compare Wakka Wakka directly to another named game
- Store keywords using another game's trademark

This is a practical originality review, not legal advice.
